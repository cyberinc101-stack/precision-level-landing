Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Precision Level ("the app") is developed and published by HappyAppsForYou. This policy explains what information the app accesses, what third-party services it uses, and how that information is handled.

Information Processed On Your Device

The level readings work without an internet connection. Accelerometer data used for roll and pitch, your calibration values, and your settings (such as sensitivity, haptic feedback, decimal places and language) are processed and stored locally on your device only. The app does not require an account, and we do not run servers that receive this data.

Camera Permission

The AR Camera Level feature requests camera access solely to display a live overlay on your screen. Camera frames are not recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. You can deny or revoke the permission in your device settings and the rest of the app will keep working.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob, including a banner ad and an ad shown when the app opens. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and general usage data. We do not receive your personal information from AdMob. How Google handles this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

In-App Purchases

The app offers an optional paid upgrade that removes ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check and manage your purchase status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information so it can confirm whether your upgrade is active and restore it on a new device. We never see or store your payment card details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy for how they handle data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google AdMob, Google Play and RevenueCat), and only to the extent needed to show ads and manage purchases.

Data Retention And Deletion

Settings and calibration data stay on your device and are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Because we do not keep your data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To ask about data held by our service providers, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
